# Code of Conduct

Transync aims to be a respectful and practical open-source project.

Participants are expected to:

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Welcome people with different levels of experience.
- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and patience.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or unwanted
  attention.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety.

Unacceptable behavior can be reported through the security contact listed in
`SECURITY.md` or through the project's issue tracker if the matter is not
sensitive. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict
participation to protect the project community.
